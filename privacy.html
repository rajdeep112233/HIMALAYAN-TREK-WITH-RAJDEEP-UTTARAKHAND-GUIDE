Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep – Privacy Notice
Website information handling

This notice describes the information requested by the website forms and the WhatsApp handoff. The website is static: its form scripts prepare a WhatsApp message but do not save form submissions in a website booking database or process payments.

1. Information You Choose to Provide

The trek and tour form requests your name, mobile number, selected experience, preferred date and participant count. Email and additional requests are optional. The separate homepage contact form requests contact details and a message. Do not include payment credentials or sensitive medical or identity information in either form.

2. WhatsApp Enquiry Handoff

When you submit a form, the site opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message addressed to the business number shown on this site. Review the message before choosing to send it. The website does not automatically transmit the message to Rajdeep, create a reservation, or confirm a booking. If you send the message, WhatsApp and its recipient receive the information in it; WhatsApp handles information under its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Purpose

Information you choose to send is used to respond to your enquiry and discuss the requested experience and its arrangements. If trip planning requires sharing details with a service provider, discuss what is needed and with whom before the details are shared.

4. Website and Third-Party Services

This website does not store form submissions in a site database. Once you send an enquiry, it is available to the WhatsApp recipient and is subject to WhatsApp's own handling and retention practices. The website also loads third-party fonts/icons and map content; those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their resources. Review their privacy information where relevant.

5. Your Choice

Submitting the form opens a message for review; you choose whether to send it. The separate homepage contact form also opens a prefilled WhatsApp message. You may contact Rajdeep without using a form if you prefer.

6. Questions and Policy Review

For questions about information you have sent, contact Rajdeep using the contact details published on this website. This notice describes the current static-site workflow and is not represented as legally reviewed; confirm applicable privacy requirements and any separate record-retention practices before relying on it as a complete legal policy.

Contact

Use the booking enquiry page or contact details on the homepage for questions about this notice.
