Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep – Privacy Policy
Updated on: 2026

At Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep, we are committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and ensuring that your personal information remains safe and secure. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you visit our website, book trekking expeditions, or use our services in Uttarakhand.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information including your name, phone number, email address, identification details, payment information, emergency contact details, and medical information when you register for treks or contact us. This information helps us manage bookings and ensure your safety during trekking expeditions.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to process trek registrations, communicate important updates, organize logistics, and provide customer support. Medical or health-related information is used strictly to ensure that all participants are safe and prepared for high-altitude trekking conditions. We may also send you information about upcoming treks, offers, and adventure programs.

3. Third-Party Disclosures

We may share limited information with trusted partners such as local guides, transport providers, accommodation partners, or medical personnel when necessary for organizing treks. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your personal information from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure. Your data is stored securely and accessed only by authorized personnel responsible for managing trek operations.

5. Consent

By using our website or booking a trek with Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of your information as described.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep reserves the right to update or modify this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be reflected with an updated effective date on this page. We encourage users to review this policy periodically.
