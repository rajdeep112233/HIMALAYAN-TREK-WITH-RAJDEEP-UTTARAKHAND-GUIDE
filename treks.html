    Fitness, safety and weather

    Difficulty is shown as previously listed and does not replace a route-specific fitness assessment. Share your trekking experience, health or accessibility needs before booking; confirm suitable preparation with the guide.

    • Check the latest local weather and official advisories before departure; mountain conditions can change quickly.
    • Confirm the day-by-day route, elevation, expected walking time and emergency arrangements.
    • Follow the guide's instructions and do not continue if conditions or your health make it unsafe.
    • Carry personal medication and tell the trip organizer about relevant medical needs.
    • Do not litter, feed wildlife or leave the agreed route without the guide's advice.
