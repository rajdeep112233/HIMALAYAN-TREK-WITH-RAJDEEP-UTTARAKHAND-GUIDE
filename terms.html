Terms & Conditions

Read these current trip conditions before sending payment. Route-specific arrangements and any missing terms must be confirmed directly before a booking is finalized.

This page summarizes booking and participation information currently published by Himalayan Trek by Rajdeep. It is not a substitute for a route-specific written confirmation or legal advice. Obtain and review the agreed itinerary, price basis, inclusions, exclusions, payment terms and cancellation conditions before making payment.

Booking and Payment

A website or WhatsApp enquiry is a request for information, not a reservation. The booking form opens a prefilled WhatsApp message for you to review and send; this static website does not save enquiries to a booking database or take online payment.

The currently published booking condition is that a booking is confirmed only after the required advance payment is received. Remaining payment is due before the trek as instructed. The advance amount, payment method, payment deadlines, and any service-specific payment conditions are not specified on this site. Confirm these, along with the itinerary, availability, price basis and inclusions in writing before paying.

Participant Safety and Preparation

Trekking and outdoor activities involve risks including injury, illness, landslides, snowfall and severe or changing weather. Participants should discuss route suitability, fitness and health concerns before confirming a trip, bring suitable personal clothing and equipment, and follow reasonable safety instructions from the guide. Do not join a route if you are not fit for its confirmed demands.

Participants should discuss relevant medical or accessibility needs directly before booking; do not send sensitive medical details through the general website form. Personal travel or adventure insurance covering trekking, medical treatment and evacuation is advised.

Cancellation and Refund Policy

The existing published terms state that refunds, if applicable, may take 10–20 working days to process. They also state that unused services and voluntary withdrawal during a trek are not refundable.

The site does not define cancellation notice deadlines, refund eligibility by notice period, deductions, or the amount/type of any advance that may be refundable. Request the cancellation terms that apply to your specific trip in writing before paying. No additional refund guarantee is made by this summary.

Rescheduling and Date Changes

The current published terms do not specify a rescheduling process, deadline, fee or guarantee. Any date change depends on availability and the conditions agreed for the trip. Contact Rajdeep before making plans to change dates, and obtain confirmation of any revised itinerary, cost and applicable cancellation conditions in writing.

Weather, Route Changes and Official Restrictions

Weather conditions, natural events, government restrictions, landslides, road blockages and safety concerns may lead to a modified, postponed or cancelled itinerary. The guide may adapt a route when conditions require it. Follow current official/local advisories and safety instructions.

The existing terms state that additional expenses arising from trip changes may be borne by the participant, but do not explain how such expenses are assessed or whether refunds apply. Clarify the process for your trip in writing before payment.

Environmental and Local Responsibility

Participants should carry out their waste, respect local communities and cultural places, follow guide instructions and applicable local conservation rules, and observe wildlife without feeding, pursuing or disturbing it. Do not leave litter or damage natural habitats.

Food, Accommodation and Transport

Food, accommodation, transportation, permits, equipment and other facilities are route- and trip-specific. Do not assume they are included unless they are expressly listed in your written trip details. Confirm any additional charges and arrangements before payment.

Conduct and Personal Belongings

Participants are responsible for their luggage, equipment and personal belongings. The existing terms prohibit alcohol or drug consumption during trekking, misbehavior with staff or other participants, and actions that threaten group safety. They state that violations may result in removal from the trek without refund.

Documentation and Insurance

Participants must carry valid identification and any required permits if applicable; route-specific requirements should be confirmed before travel. Personal travel or adventure insurance covering trekking, emergency evacuation and medical treatment is advised. The site does not provide insurance.

Photographs and Media

The previously published terms state that participants grant permission for trip photographs or videos to be used for promotional purposes. Confirm this media-use condition and the consent process before booking; do not assume that a participant's image will be used without clear permission. This clause requires business confirmation and legal review before reliance.

Complaints and Disputes

The previously published terms requested that complaints be raised within seven days of the trip and referred to Indian law and courts in Uttarakhand. The appropriate deadline, dispute process and jurisdiction should be confirmed and reviewed by a qualified legal professional before these terms are relied upon.

Questions and Communication

For route, payment or policy questions, contact Rajdeep through the booking enquiry page or the contact details published on this website. Keep written confirmation of trip-specific arrangements and payment terms.
